# Clean Workflow artwork

The **Fit** symbol uses two complementary pieces and an open stepped passage.
It suggests work moving through stages and being refined into alignment. Pair
it with the name **Clean Workflow** while establishing recognition; the symbol
does not attempt to explain every feature.

## Files

| Asset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `icon.svg` | White symbol on a green tile; composer icon on either appearance |
| `logo.svg` | Green transparent symbol for light backgrounds |
| `logo-dark.svg` | Mint transparent symbol for dark backgrounds |
| `logo-black.svg` | Single-colour dark artwork on light backgrounds |
| `logo-white.svg` | Single-colour reversed artwork on dark backgrounds |

All masters are 256 × 256 SVGs with filled paths, real negative space,
accessible titles, and no fonts, external resources, embedded rasters, filters,
or scripts. Their geometry matches the approved option C. The 32-unit passage
preserves separation at small sizes; the outer corners have a 12-unit radius.

## Palette and sizing

| Colour | HEX | RGB | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workflow green | `#227A64` | 34, 122, 100 | Primary symbol and icon tile |
| Mint | `#72D7B4` | 114, 215, 180 | Symbol on dark backgrounds |
| Ink | `#111111` | 17, 17, 17 | Dark monochrome |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Reversed symbol |

Use at least 16 px for the complete square asset; prefer 24 px or larger where
space allows. Preserve its proportions and built-in clear space. For a larger
brand placement, leave at least one passage width around the visible symbol.
Keep the title in the host's normal interface typography; do not squeeze words
inside the symbol. No custom typeface is required.

Use the tiled icon on mixed or unknown backgrounds. Use `logo-dark.svg` or
`logo-white.svg` on dark backgrounds rather than the dark monochrome variant.
Do not stretch, rotate, close the passage, add outlines or shadows, or assign
different colours to the two pieces. This is a screen-first identity; print
colour matching and physical production have not been verified.
